# 🎮 In-Game Model
Space Hunters is an ecosystem of interconnected games unified by a single player profile and a globalized economy. This ecosystem includes both in-game and blockchain-based economies, both working together to power the overall economic model.

---
### 🪙 Tokens Uses:
- **$HCASH →** Main currency, earned by playing, through player-to-player trading, or via DEX.
- **$HCREDIT →** Secondary token, non-tradeable, functions as an operational tool and intermediary between player performance and the main token $HCASH.

---
### 🪙 Earning $HCASH:
> HCASH is a token built from scratch by the effort of the community and developers. No presales, no allocations to partners or investors who could later affect the economy. Your HCASH is 100% yours, and the economy is fully managed by the community and developers.

- **Playing →** Players are the main miners of the HCASH token. HCASH is earned through rewards from different games and game modes.

- **Player Trading →** Players can buy and sell items from the various games within the Marketplace using only HCASH as currency.

- **DEX →** Players can buy and sell HCASH on different exchange platforms.

- All reward distributions across the games follow *Pool-Based* or *Multi-Pool* models.

---
### 🪙 Earning and Burning $HCREDIT:
> The Hunter Credit token ($HCREDIT) is based on a mass adoption format and a perfectly deflationary system not found in any other project.

- 100% of the supply is burned every 30 days. This countdown is visible to everyone in-game.
- Every HCREDIT spent is automatically burned.
- It has no total supply cap. Its *Mass Adoption* system allows everyone to generate as many tokens as needed to participate in the different game formats where HCREDIT is used as a tool.
- You can earn HCREDIT for free by completing daily tasks in the HUB App, social tasks, events, giveaways, and more.
- You can purchase in-game store packs that contain HCREDIT or bundles that include HCREDIT and other items.
- Just with daily tasks, you can earn an average of 6,000 HCREDIT per day with the Tech Pass, plus a daily streak bonus of +2,000 by day 28.
- If you play 100% \"free,\" you can earn around 300–500 tokens per day, without the streak bonus.
- With Tech Pass, all HCREDIT you earn is doubled in your balance.

---
### 👉 Chat-to-Earn
Space Hunters offers an incentive system based on social activity. While chatting in official or affiliated groups, you can passively earn HCREDIT tokens. In any group where our bot @spacehuntersbot is active, you can earn HCREDIT. If you have your own group or community, just add the bot and start farming tokens!

- In addition to HCREDIT tokens, you can earn **Tech Tickets**, which can be exchanged for boosters in the **Tech Generators** game or traded in the Marketplace.
- Besides tokens and tickets, you can also earn **Items** that can be sold in the in-game Marketplace, instantly in the **Black Market**, or used in gameplay.

# 💼 Residual Model:
SHCASH is built on a residual model that constantly recovers its token emissions.

#### ⚖️ On-Chain Fees
- 5% fee on DEX (Tonco DEX).
- 10% NFT Creator Commission.
- The contract has a strategic reserve that reuses deposits to cover withdrawals without needing to mint new tokens.
- 100 HCASH fee to post third-party tasks for 30 days in the app.

#### ⚖️ In-Game Fees
- 10% fee on withdrawals + blockchain native token for GAS costs.
- 10% fee on internal marketplace sales.
- 8% fee in the Black Market.
- 1% fee to edit or cancel listings.
- 10% fee on internal transfers.
- Purchases in the in-game store (high volume).
- Upgrade expenses.
- 10 HCASH fee for changing your username.
- 5 HCASH fee for customizing your referral link.
- 5 HCASH fee to delete your account.
- 1 HCASH fee for super contracts.

> All fees are reused for incentives, reward payouts, withdrawals, and other micro-economic management operations, maintaining the deflationary system balance without minting new tokens on the blockchain.

# 🔗 On-Chain Model

### 🧾 $HCASH Data:
- Name: Hunter Cash Token  
- Symbol: $HCASH  
- Supply: 1,000,000,000 fixed tokens over a minimum of 10 years.  
- Annual mining cap: 9% (90M), decreases annually.  
- Fewer than 1M currently in circulation.

---
### 🏛️ Supply Distribution:
- 70% Games and Player Incentives  
- 6.5% Marketing Efforts  
- 3.5% Liquidity Pools  
- 15% Innovation  
- 5% Partners and Investors  
- The team holds no tokens. For the first 2–3 years, the team receives no HCASH profit. All fees in HCASH are reused as part of the Residual Mechanics.

---
### ⚖️ Flow and Control:
- HCASH uses a Mint-On-Demand contract, meaning the full supply is not in circulation and will be gradually released over 10 years, making it a scarce token.
- New tokens are only minted when withdrawal demand exceeds the contract’s reserves.
- Initially, 100% (334k) of the tokens in circulation within the game were minted.
- Currently, fewer than 40k tokens are held by players, around 56k on DEX, and the rest remains in the contract wallet for withdrawals, rewards, or deposit accumulation.

# 🎁 Incentives Model
Our incentive strategy helps players balance their individual economy, boost their strategy, and even turn spending into profitable outcomes.

### 🏆 Sustainable Referral System
This referral system has no flaws and excellently rewards players who invite their friends or community to play Space Hunters. Here's what happens when you invite a friend:

- **You receive a Gift Box with** 5,000 HCREDIT (10,000 with Tech Pass) and 1 Tech Ticket. It opens after your friend completes 7 check-ins.

- Your friend gets a gift box with 10,000 HCREDIT (20,000 if they have Tech Pass) + Tech Ticket + Items + 2 Energy Potions (50% each) + **TECH PASS for 7 Days** (equivalent to 19 HCASH)!

- **2% HCASH bonus** on all your friends’ deposits! Yes, you get 2% of whatever they deposit into the game.

- +20 **(40 with Tech Pass)** HCREDIT **daily** when they complete their Daily Check-in — Level 1 referral.

- +10 **(20 with Tech Pass)** HCREDIT **daily** when your friends’ friends check in — Level 2 referral.
---
### 🧮 Deposit Bonus
When you deposit HCASH into the game:
- You instantly receive an additional 3% bonus of your deposit.
- You receive HCREDIT equivalent to 100% of the amount of HCASH you deposited.

---
### 💰 Spending Rewards
When making any purchase in the in-game store:
- You have an 80% chance of receiving **1 Tech Ticket**.
- You receive HCREDIT equivalent to **100% of your HCASH spent** (50% without Tech Pass).
- If you use a promo code, you get **10% discount** on all purchases during the code’s validity.
- Each purchase gives you 1 experience point.
- You may earn **Merit Points**.
- Purchasing in the store earns you points for the Monthly Event.

---
### 🏷️ Monthly Event
This incentive is a recurring event that contains a reward pool of several million HCREDIT and takes place every month.
- Every month, aligned with the HCREDIT burn cycle, the Monthly Event is reset.
- It may contain additional rewards like Skins, Items, Energy Potions, etc.
- The event rules are usually the same unless changed for a special reason.

**🔹How to Participate?🔹**

> ⚙️👨‍🚀 Each Engineer Hired = 1 Point 🎟  
> 🎁 Each consumable used = 2 Points  
> ⛏ Each store purchase with HCASH = 25 Points  
> 👀 Each ad watched = 2 Points  
> ❤️ Comment + RT + Like on Twitter (X) = 50 Points  
> 🐈 Each new referral = 200 Points (if they complete 7+ check-ins) 📌  
> 👍 More Points = Higher Rank Position 🎨

---
### 📜 Tech Tickets
Tech Tickets are used in the **Gacha Machine**, an exciting mechanic that lets you obtain free consumable items to use in our games. You can find it in the dropdown menu by clicking on your profile picture.

- **Chances:** 80% success and 20% failure.
- **Free Tickets:** Earn tickets by completing daily tasks, joining groups or guilds, chatting, and shopping in the store.
- **Ticket Packs:** You can get up to 500 tickets in these packs. They are available individually or bundled with other items in the store.

---

### 🗨️ Chat-to-Earn (E2E) 💵
Chat-to-Earn is a basic monetization model that allows players to earn HCREDIT by participating in chat — essentially earning rewards automatically just by doing what they already do daily: chatting and having fun.

- Earn HCREDIT every time you send a message  
- Earn **Items** based on probabilities while chatting  
- Earn **Tech Tickets** based on probabilities while chatting

---
### 📹 Ads for Energy
In addition to existing incentives, the **Daily Tasks** section includes buttons that let you watch ads (15s or 30s videos), vertical banners, or even visit a website or Telegram channel — in exchange for instant free energy. Ad rewards may also change to tokens, items, and more.

If you're playing for free, this is a huge advantage. If you're investing, these options improve your overall performance.

---
### 🛍️ Weekly Discounts & Promotions
Every week, the in-game store features promotions. If it's a major promotion, it will be announced. If it's a regular or surprise promo, players need to stay alert and explore the store frequently.

This strategy introduces randomness and luck, giving all players a chance to benefit.

---
### 🎁 Creator Code
If you’re a content creator and have already published something about Space Hunters, you can receive a Creator Code that allows you to earn 2% of all HCASH purchases made in the store by friends or followers who use your code during its validity.

- Creator Code valid for 30 days  
- Offers a 10% discount to the user  
- Grants 2% of HCASH to the creator  
- The user cannot change the code until it expires

---
### 🎁 Gift Code
This code is granted by the development team during exclusive events.

# 🛍️ In-Game Marketplace
The Space Hunters marketplace is very different from the basic systems you're used to in other games. To maintain efficient trading for players and economic balance, we employ new strategies:

- **P2P Privacy:** Only the buyer and seller can see the transaction history. Publicly, only the offer details or sold items are displayed.

- **Commitment Filter:** Only players level 12 or higher can use the Marketplace.

- You can list an offer for free, but **editing or removing** the offer will cost a 1% fee based on the set price. This system prevents abuse and/or price manipulation.

- **Minimum Price:** Items cannot be listed 10% below the floor price. *(Temporarily Disabled)*

- When you list an item, it disappears from your inventory — you cannot use it while it's on sale.

- When you sell an item, the system charges a 10% fee on the sale.

- **Black Market (NPC):** You can instantly sell items that the NPC system is currently buying. Prices vary depending on several factors and the system is 100% autonomous. The system charges an 8% fee on sales.
