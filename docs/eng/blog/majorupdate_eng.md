### **The Gacha Machine** 

![alt text](/static/img/gacha.png)

The **Gacha Machine** is an exciting mechanic that allows you to obtain free consumable items to use in **Tech: Generators**. You can find it in the drop-down menu by clicking on your profile picture.
#### **Main features:**
- **Probabilities:** 80% success and 20% failure.
- **Free tickets:** You can obtain tickets by completing daily tasks, participating in groups and guilds, chatting, and buying in the shop (with an 80% probability of receiving a ticket per purchase).
### **Use of Consumable Items**  
Before using consumable items, it is important to know the correct process to apply them properly:  
1. **Access the workshop:**  
   - Below the ship map, you will find a button called **"Engineers"**.  
   - By tapping it, you will enter the workshop section (Alpha), where you will see two tabs:  
    Inventario:** Shows the available consumables for use.  
    Supplies:** A mini-shop within the game where you can buy more supplies without closing the game.  
2. **Consumables application:**  
   - To use an item with a **direct** effect (applied to a specific generator), you must first select the generator at the top of the screen.  
    Important:** If you do not have engineers hired in generators, the consumable **will not take effect** and will be lost. Make sure to hire engineers before applying them.  
3. **Types of effects:**  
   - Some items have **global** effects, affecting **all** generators or engineers.  
   - Others have **direct** effects, impacting only a specific generator.  
   - Certain items are designed to **counteract negative effects** and will only work if the engineers or generators are under the influence of such an anomaly.

### **Black Market** 

The **Black Market** is a new mechanic designed to offer an alternative **play-to-earn**, allowing users to quickly monetize some of their items. This feature was initially launched with the 4 basic elements of the **Chat-Adventure** game.
#### **How it works:**
- **Sale in groups of 100:** You can only sell items if you have at least 100 units. If you have less, you will have to wait until you reach that amount.
- **Price per unit:** The price shown is per unit, not for the entire group.
- **Dynamic prices:** The price varies according to the circulating supply of the item.
- **Reference graphs:** The graph shows the current supply and its variation compared to the previous day.
- **Commission (fee):** The Black Market has a fixed commission of **8%** on each transaction.

### **Marketplace Mechanics** 
This Marketplace is unique and was specifically designed to protect your assets. Below, we explain the rules that ensure fair and balanced trading.

#### **Rules for protection against "price dumping":**
1. **Minimum price limit:** You cannot set a price lower than **10% of the Floor Price** of the same item.
2. **Protection against the official shop:** If the item is available in the shop, you cannot set a price lower than **50% of the shop price**.
3. **Sale fee:** Listing an item for sale is free, but once sold, the system will deduct a **10%** commission.
4. **Cancellation fee:** If you decide to cancel a listing, the system will take a **1%** of the previous price.
5. **Price adjustment fee:** Each time you modify the price of an item, the system will deduct a **1%** of the previous price.

### **Energy and Experience** 
Energy and experience are two new features that we have activated in this update. As many of you know, these are two very important features in your unique shared experience profile, as the level you achieve and the experience you earn works across all our games. Let's start by discussing the details:
#### **Energy:**
Not all actions in the ecosystem will consume energy, but many of them do to complement balance and eliminate exploitation in any possible aspect, but also promote a solid foundation of work distribution among different activities. It is important that you understand how to efficiently distribute your energy to optimize your performance.
Here are data on the activities that currently consume energy:
- **Chat-Adventure expeditions:** 8 energy points.
- **Hiring Engineers:** 1 energy point. Not per engineer, but per action. That is, if you hire 5 engineers together, that counts as 1 action. If you later go and hire 2 more, that counts as another action.
- **Turning on the Generator in cycle 11:** Consumes 2 energy points, but if you have Tech Pass, it will only be 1 energy point. This consumption occurs only in cycle 11.
For now, these are all the actions that consume energy, super simple and basic to introduce this mechanic into the ecosystem.
#### **Experience:**
Experience is basic and necessary to unlock many things in the project, specifically reaching the trust level for withdrawals, which would be level 15. The activities that will provide experience in this first update are:
- **Daily tasks:** Grant 1 experience point each time you complete a task. If you have Tech Pass, you get 5 points.
- **If you have Tech Pass, each purchase in the shop gives you an experience point.**
- **Hiring engineers:** 2 points and with Tech Pass, 8 experience points.
- **Turning on the generator:** Grants 5 experience points and with Tech Pass, 10 points. Same as energy, this happens only when reaching cycle 11.
- **Expanding the generator (Slots):** Grants 1 experience point and with Tech Pass, 5 points, per slot.
- **In Chat-Adventure Expeditions, upon returning with any material, you gain experience:**
  - **Yggdrasil Wood:** 2 experience points.
  - **Celestite Stone:** 15 experience points.
  - **Orichalcum Metal:** 150 experience points.
These are all the activities for now. We will integrate more as we advance in development.

#### **Anomalies:**

- There are **41 different types of anomalies**.
- Anomalies can last up to **12 consecutive hours**.
- They have negative or positive effects on one or more generators, affecting their base efficiency between **10% and 30%**.
- After an anomaly, there is a **50% probability** of experiencing side effects (**Side Effects**) that can be positive or negative.

#### **Mechanics of Anomalies**

![alt text](/static/img/anomalia.png)

The system will notify in the main chat when an anomaly is about to occur, alerting players about its possible impact. In the game, a mini radar will show the name of the anomaly and the types of generators that will be affected, either positively or negatively.

By interacting with the yellow alert symbol on the affected generators, a window with detailed information will pop up, including:

- **Side effects:** If applicable, both during and after the anomaly.
- **Current effects:** Consumables applied by the player or other participants.
#### **Color coding in the effects window:**
- 🟢 **Green:** Indicates an anomaly with positive effects.
- 🔴 **Red:** Represents an anomaly with negative effects.
- 🔵 **Blue:** Signals the presence of a side effect.
- 🟣 **Purple:** Identifies a part equipped in the generator.
- 🟡 **Yellow:** Indicates consumables applied by the player.
- ⚫ **Gray:** Shows consumables applied by another user.

The algorithm we are developing for anomalies works with many data points seeking to simulate intelligence, so it will improve in practice. We aim for it not to be abusive and to become quite random, simulating the creation of events in a natural realistic way. That is, we will try to ensure it does not affect any generator negatively in a suffocating way nor favor any generator excessively in a continuous manner.

#### **Generator Upgrades:**

![alt text](/static/img/upgrade-engineer.png)

To upgrade the generators, you need to enter the **Upgrade** option below the ship map and follow these steps:
1. Select the generator you want to upgrade.
2. Tap the central tab **"Equipped"**.
3. Buy additional slots to expand the mechanical capacity of the generator. The price of slots increases exponentially with each expansion to maintain balanced gameplay. All generators have slot prices according to their original base power. Slots are permanent.
4. Equip parts obtained in **Parts Pack** to improve the generator's hashrate.

##### **Available parts:**

- **Hash Basic:** Provides +12 Hashrate (0.012).
- **Hash Plus:** Provides +50 Hashrate (0.050).
- **Hash Rotor:** Provides +180 Hashrate (0.180).
These parts can be obtained by opening **Parts Pack**. You can buy these packs in the game shop. Parts obtained are based on probability.
You can also expand the capacity of engineers working on your generator with the **Crew Contracts** available in the shop; these increase the engineer capacity by **+12**.
- **All these upgrades are permanent and transferable when changing ownership.**
- **Equipped parts cannot be unequipped, only burned.**
- **Soon we will add a function to combine 4 identical parts to create one of the next level.** This helps to create powerful pieces occupying less space and reusing the parts you got in your packs or unwanted parts from other players obtained in the market.
- **There is no upgrade limit, you can upgrade as much as you want.**

#### **Generator Delegation:**

![alt text](/static/img/delegate.png)
This update introduces a new mechanic of shared work, which we call **"Delegation"**. In simple terms, it involves assigning a player the role of Operator and having them handle operating your generator, that is, turning it on during the established time in the contract and while doing so, they receive a percentage of the profits generated by your generator.

##### **Details:**

- Both **Owner** and **Operator** must have Tech Pass to delegate.
- The owner sets the contract details.
- The proposal is sent to the applicant Operator and must be accepted or rejected.
- If the applicant operator does not respond to the proposal, it must be canceled if you want to send it to someone else.
- If the proposal is accepted and the contract takes effect according to the date conditions, the generator will no longer appear as yours and the new operator will see it on the ship map in blue as if it were theirs.
- The energy costs, operational cost (10 HCREDIT per ignition), and experience will be handled by the current operator.
- Profits are automatically divided according to the established percentage.
- If the operator does not violate the contract rules, the owner has no option to cancel.
- If the operator violates the rules, the owner will see the option to cancel the contract within the delegation options.

##### **Step-by-step of delegation:**

To delegate, you must tap inside your generator the option to the right of the ignition button, then set the terms:

- **Operator Username (HUB Username):** The operator's username.
- **Round:** Number of blocks they will work.
- **Fails:** How many times you will allow them to fail in ignition.
- **Inactivity:** The delay time you consider acceptable for turning on the generator. After that time, even if they turn it on, it is considered a **"Fail"**.
- **Dates:** Determines when you want the contract to start and end, it must be according to the minimum number of blocks. You can even schedule in advance when you want them to start working.
- **Profits:** Set the percentage you want to give them of your profits with that generator.
That said, I think everything is clear. The rest is putting it into practice to master the mechanic.
