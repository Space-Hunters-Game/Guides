### **The HUB Shop** 
The shop within the HUB is a centralized space where you can find items from all the games and expansions in our ecosystem. Although for now we only play **Tech: Generators**, it is important to highlight that this shop is designed to grow along with the ecosystem.

### **Black Market** 
The **Black Market** is a new mechanic designed to offer a **play-to-earn** alternative, allowing users to quickly monetize some of their items. This function was initially launched with the 4 basic elements of the game **Chat-Adventure**.
#### **How it works:**
- **Selling in groups of 100:** You can only sell items if you have at least 100 units. If you have less, you will need to wait until you reach that amount.
- **Price per unit:** The displayed price is per unit, not for the entire group.
- **Dynamic prices:** The price varies according to the circulating supply of the item.
- **Reference graphs:** The graph shows the current supply and its variation compared to the previous day.
- **Commission (fee):** The Black Market has a fixed commission of **8%** on each transaction.
- To sell on the Marketplace , you must first combine 100 identical elements into a new item called a "Pot" or "Box" , which represents the 100 units. You can do this from the inventory by tapping the "Action" button and then selecting "Bulk" . If you want to reverse the process, follow the same steps, but instead of selecting "Bulk" , choose "Split" to break the item back into its individual units.

### **Marketplace HUB** 
The **Marketplace HUB** is a centralized market where you can buy and sell all items from all games in our ecosystem. To facilitate the experience, we have implemented filters that allow you to browse and find what you need quickly.
#### **How to sell an item:**
1. Go to your **Inventory**.
2. Select the item you wish to sell.
   - If you see the **"Sell"** button, click directly.
   - If you see **"Action"**, click on that button first and then select **"Sell"**.
3. Enter the sale price and confirm by clicking **"Sell"** again.

### **Marketplace Mechanics** 
This Marketplace is unique and was specifically designed to protect your assets. Below, we explain the rules that ensure fair and balanced trading.
#### **Rules against "price dumping":**
1. **Minimum price limit:** You cannot set a price lower than **10% of the Floor Price** of the same item.
2. **Protection against the official shop:** If the item is available in the shop, you cannot set a price lower than **50% of the shop price**.
3. **Sale fee:** Listing an item for sale has no cost, but once sold, the system will deduct **10%** as commission.
4. **Cancellation fee:** If you decide to cancel a listing, the system will take **1%** of the price you set.
5. **Price adjustment fee:** Each time you modify the price of an item, the system will deduct **1%** of the new price.

### **Tech: Generators** 
#### **Exit button:**
We added a button for an immediate exit from the game to the HUB, improving the user experience and saving time.
#### **Anomalies:**
- There are **41 different types of anomalies**.
- Anomalies can last up to **12 consecutive hours**.
- They have negative or positive effects on one or more generators, affecting their base efficiency between **10% and 30%**.
- After an anomaly, there is a **50% probability** of experiencing side effects (**Side Effects**) that can be positive or negative.
#### **Generator Upgrade:**
To upgrade generators, you must enter the **Upgrade** option below the ship map and follow these steps:
1. Select the generator you wish to upgrade.
2. Tap the central tab **"Equipped"**.
3. Buy additional slots to expand the mechanical capacity of the generator.
4. Equip parts obtained in **Parts Pack** to improve the generator's hashrate.
