## **About** <a name="about"></a>
**Tech: Generators** is a Telegram Mini App blockchain-powered strategy game where players manage futuristic energy generators, train specialized engineers, and trade resources in a competitive yet collaborative environment. Set in a world driven by advanced technology and interdependent civilizations, players must optimize their operations, explore technological upgrades, and collaborate across races to thrive in a dynamic, ever-evolving ecosystem.

In short, this game is a perfect Sci-Fi **$HCASH token mining simulation** converted into an addictive web3 game with a complex algorithm that gives you choices to create different strategies to maximize your earnings per block. Totally unique and with no competitors in the sector thanks to our own algorithm, which balances and adjusts in real-time every single power fluctuation caused by Space Anomalies, consumables, permanent effects, side effects, player contributions, number of engineers, and many other factors.

## **What Can Players Do?** <a name="what-can-players-do"></a>
### **Manage Generators**
Players own and operate generators with unique attributes and characteristics, requiring careful planning to balance production cycles, maintenance, and rest periods. These generators are extremely limited and unique in the web3 market, equivalent to owning land or even more powerful assets.

### **Contract Engineers**
Players can contract engineers and assign them to work on generators. Engineers can be trained to become more efficient. Initially common, engineers gain value as they learn special abilities and can eventually be traded (coming soon) after meeting certain criteria.

### **More Coming Soon**
The next expansion, **Tech: Recyclers**, will introduce additional features and gameplay mechanics.

## **All About Generators** <a name="all-about-generators"></a>

### **Buying Generators** <a name="buying-generators"></a>
- You can purchase generators in the store of our app (**HUB App**) or in the marketplace from other players.
- The **Generator Boxes** in the store provide random generators when opened.
- There are **25 different generators**, each with unique characteristics.
- Users can own up to **1 generator** without a subscription, but with the monthly **TechPass**, they can own as many as they can acquire.

### **Generator Owner Rules** <a name="generator-owner-rules"></a>
- Generator owners cannot hire engineers for their own generators.
- Each generator has a **base power** that can be improved with the help of engineers, events, and additional effects from temporary or permanent in-game items.
- You can generate hourly income without engineers.
- The earnings are split **35/65**, where:
  - **35%** is always for the generator owner.
  - **65%** is split among engineers. Without engineers, the 65% portion is returned to the game treasury.
- Generators are limited, so make sure to acquire them soon. Availability depends on the average daily number of active players.
- Generator owners must keep their generator turned on to produce power and generate income for both themselves and the engineers assigned to that generator.
- If the generator is off, it does not generate income.
- Generators can be upgraded with expandable slots and equipped parts, consumable items, or special items.
- Base Power: Each generator has a base power that contributes to the total reward.
- Additional Power: The generator's power increases with the assignment of engineers. The more engineers assigned, the greater the boost. Bonus items activated by engineers can often stack, further enhancing the generator's capacity.
- Generator owners can share their generator number so their friends can find it and support each other.
- The generator owner can delegate the task of turning it on each hour to a specific user if they possess a **Tech Pass**. Owners can delegate to different operators if they own more than one generator, but one operator can only be delegated by one owner.

## **Engineers** <a name="engineers"></a>
- Engineers are purchased with **$HCREDIT** and remain assigned to a generator for **24 hours**.
- The 24-hour countdown is individual for each unit (unit = 1 engineer), meaning they don’t leave all at once but in the order they were hired as their respective 24 hours expire.
- You can assign engineers to generators to increase their power and your rewards.
- You can assign up to **5 engineers per generator** without a subscription, with no limit on the number of generators.
- With the monthly **TechPass**, you can assign up to **20 engineers per generator**.
- Assigned engineers cannot be removed.
- If you assign engineers to a generator that is working, but the owner fails to keep it running during its rounds, the downtime will not generate income for you. Make sure to use generators owned by reliable players.
- The power provided by 1 engineer is **10 Khash** and can be boosted with temporary and permanent items.

## **Items and Upgrades** <a name="items-and-upgrades"></a>
- If you own generators, you can upgrade them by purchasing slots and equipping **Hash Parts**.
  - Slot cost increases with every purchase by **4.2%**, and the starting cost depends on which generator you own. This mechanism ensures fairness between generator classes based on their base power.
- If you don’t own generators, you can still use **consumable items** (owners can use them too), which provide temporary effects depending on which item you use, and many of them can stack the effect (effect duration cannot be stacked).
- There are **3 different equipped items (parts)**:
  - **Hash Basic**: 12 permanent base power.
  - **Hash Plus**: 50 permanent base power.
  - **Hash Rotor**: 180 permanent base power.
  - These equipped items cannot be unequipped. If you want to remove them, they get burned. You can combine 4 of the same item to create 1 of the next rarity.
- You can get free items from **Tech Tickets**. Tickets can be earned by completing tasks (**10% chance**), purchasing in the store (**80% chance**), chatting in our groups, or completing social tasks.
- You can purchase tickets and parts packs from our game store or marketplace.
